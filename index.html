01 · Power BI dashboard

02 · M.Sc. thesis

Predicting ICU Readmissions

The question

03 · M.Sc. capstone project

Explainable AI for Cancer Mutations

The question
Can a transparent model predict the clinical impact of cancer mutations, so clinicians can see why it decides?
Data
3,321 expert-labeled tumor mutations (MSKCC, Kaggle) with linked biomedical literature; ClinVar and COSMIC used for biological validation.
My approach
Encoded genes and mutations, turned clinical text into features (TF-IDF), handled class imbalance, compared logistic regression, random forest and XGBoost, explained predictions with SHAP, and built an interactive app.
Result
XGBoost performed best across 9 imbalanced classes (accuracy 0.66, macro-F1 0.53 vs. 0.48 for logistic regression, macro AUC 0.90). SHAP showed biologically meaningful patterns: truncating mutations pointed to loss of function, fusion events to gain of function.
Tools
Pythonscikit-learnXGBoostSHAPStreamlit

06 · M.Sc. team project

Human vs. AI Art: Emotional Perception Study

The question
Can AI-generated art evoke the same creativity and emotional response as human art?
Data
170 human and AI artwork pairs across four genres, and a perception survey answered by 150 people.
My approach
Co-built the paired dataset, designed the survey and automated pair rotation with Google Apps Script (952 survey versions), analyzed the responses in Power BI, coded open answers into themes, and interpreted and wrote up the perception results.
Result
Participants spotted the AI artwork in 91% of cases and rated human art as more creative (61%), yet reported slightly higher emotional connection with AI art (3.05 vs. 2.82 on a 1–7 scale).
Tools
Google FormsGoogle Apps ScriptPower BI
